The Impact of Remote Work on Organizational Productivity: A Longitudinal Study

Abstract
This paper examines the relationship between remote work arrangements and
measured productivity outcomes across 340 organizations over a five-year period.

Introduction
Since 2020, remote work has fundamentally altered how organizations structure
labor. Prior research suggests mixed effects on output across sectors.

Methodology
We employed a mixed-methods approach combining survey data with productivity
metrics collected from participating organizations across multiple industries.

Results
Our findings indicate a statistically significant correlation between hybrid
work models and self-reported job satisfaction, though productivity effects
varied considerably by industry sector and organizational size.

Conclusion
Further research is needed to establish causal mechanisms behind these effects.
