ERA Applicant User Guide

Introduction
This guide explains how to use the Employee Retirement Application ERA portal.
Applicants should follow the process outlined below to submit their application.

Getting Started
To begin, applicants must create an account. The experience of using this system
should take approximately fifteen to twenty minutes for most users.

Submitting Your Application
Once you have completed the required fields, click submit. Your application will
then enter the review process. You will receive a confirmation once complete.

Frequently Asked Questions
What if I make an error. You can edit your application before final submission.
How long does review take. Typically five to ten business days depending on volume.
